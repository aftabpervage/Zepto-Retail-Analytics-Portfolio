# 🛒 Zepto Inventory & Sales Analysis Dashboard  

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project is an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze **sales performance, inventory availability, discounts, and category-wise insights** from a retail dataset.  

The dashboard helps in understanding:  
- Revenue contribution by categories and products  
- Inventory availability (In-Stock vs Out-Of-Stock)  
- Discount trends across categories  
- Business performance insights through interactive visualizations  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used  
- Power BI Desktop – Dashboard creation & visualization  
- Power Query – Data cleaning & transformation  
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations & measures  
- SQL – Initial data preparation and querying  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  

### KPI Cards  
- Total Revenue  
- In-Stock %  
- Out-Of-Stock %  
- Average Discount %  
- Total Categories  

### Interactive Visualizations  
- Revenue by Category  
- Total Revenue by Product Name  
- Products by Category  
- Average Discount % by Category  
- Stock Availability Analysis  
- Slicers for Category, Product Name, and Stock Status

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- Total revenue generated from the dataset is **224.15M**.  
- Cooking Essentials and Munchies categories contribute the highest revenue.  
- Fruits & Vegetables category provides the highest average discount percentage.  
- More than **87%** of products are currently in stock, indicating strong inventory availability.  
- Around **12%** of products are out of stock, showing efficient inventory management.  
- Revenue is concentrated among top-selling products such as **Borges Extra Light Olive Oil** and **Dhara products**.  
- Categories like Home & Cleaning generate comparatively lower revenue and may require promotional strategies.  
- Discount percentages vary significantly across categories, indicating category-specific pricing strategies.  
- The dashboard supports interactive analysis using slicers for category, stock status, and product names.
